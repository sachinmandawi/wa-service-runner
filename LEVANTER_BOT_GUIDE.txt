1. PRIVACY & SPY FEATURES (Enable / Disable)
---------------------------------------------
A. Anti-Delete (Delete huye message dekhna)
   - ENABLE (Personal Chat me aaye):  .delete p
   - DISABLE (Band karna):            .delete off

B. Anti-Edit (Edit hone se pehle ka message dekhna)
   - ENABLE (Personal Chat me aaye):  .antiedit p
   - DISABLE (Band karna):            .antiedit off

C. Auto Status View (Sabke Status Khud Dekhna)
   - ENABLE (Auto-Seen On):           .status on
   - DISABLE (Auto-Seen Off):         .status off

D. AFK Mode (Away From Keyboard / Busy Status)
   - ENABLE (Busy Mode On):           .afk Main abhi busy hoon, baad me baat karta hoon.
   - DISABLE (Busy Mode Off):         .afk off
     (Ya koi bhi message bhejne se AFK apne aap OFF ho jata hai)

E. Secret Target Status Tracker (Option B: Sirf 1-2 logon ke Status track karna)
   - KYA KARTA HAI:
     Target contact (jaise Girlfriend) jab bhi koi status lagakar DELETE karegi,
     ya jab 24 ghante poore hone par status EXPIRE hokar hatega —
     bot us photo, video ya text ko caption ke sath direct aapke PERSONAL DM me bhej dega!
   - CHECK / LIST KAREIN:    .trackstatus
   - TARGET ADD KAREIN:      .trackstatus add 994402551176@s.whatsapp.net
     (Tip: Kisi ke bhi message par Swipe/Reply karke '.trackstatus add' bhi likh sakte hain)
   - TARGET HATAYEIN:        .trackstatus del 994402551176@s.whatsapp.net
   - TEST KAREIN:            .trackstatus test
   (Default Target: 994402551176@s.whatsapp.net pehle se tracked hai!)


2. MESSAGE SCHEDULER (.setschedule)
---------------------------------------------
* Formula: .setschedule JID, Minute-Hour-Tareekh-Mahina, once
* Note: Kisi bhi message/photo par REPLY (Swipe) karke command bhejni hai.
* Rule: Number ke peeche @s.whatsapp.net lagana zaroori hai!
  (Example: 994402551176@s.whatsapp.net)

A. SINGLE PERSON KO BHEJNA:
   - Kal Subah 7:00 AM (9 Oct):
     .setschedule 994402551176@s.whatsapp.net, 0-7-9-10, once

   - Kal Raat 11:00 PM (9 Oct):
     .setschedule 994402551176@s.whatsapp.net, 0-23-9-10, once

   - Parso Subah 7:00 AM (10 Oct):
     .setschedule 994402551176@s.whatsapp.net, 0-7-10-10, once

   - ROZ Subah 7:00 AM Daily (Good Morning):
     .setschedule 994402551176@s.whatsapp.net, 0-7

   - ROZ Raat 11:00 PM Daily (Good Night):
     .setschedule 994402551176@s.whatsapp.net, 0-23

B. EK SE ZYADA (MULTIPLE) LOGON KO EK SATH SCHEDULE KARNA:
   (Sabhi JIDs ke beech me comma ',' lagana hai):
   - Kal Subah 7:00 AM par 3 logon ko:
     .setschedule 994402551176@s.whatsapp.net, 919876543210@s.whatsapp.net, 918765432109@s.whatsapp.net, 0-7-9-10, once

   - Roz Subah 7:00 AM par 2 logon ko:
     .setschedule 994402551176@s.whatsapp.net, 919876543210@s.whatsapp.net, 0-7

C. CHECK KAISE KAREIN:
   .getschedule

D. DISABLE / CANCEL KAISE KAREIN:
   .delschedule all


3. STATUS SCHEDULER (.scstatus)
---------------------------------------------
* Formula: .scstatus [Audience] | Minute-Hour-Tareekh-Mahina
* Note: Kisi photo, video ya text par REPLY (Swipe) karke chalana hai.

A. SET / ENABLE KAISE KAREIN:
   - Sabhi Contacts ke liye (Kal Subah 7:00 AM):
     .scstatus contact|0-7-9-10

   - Secret Status - Sirf Girlfriend ke liye (Kal Subah 7:00 AM):
     .scstatus 994402551176@s.whatsapp.net|0-7-9-10

   - Secret Status - 10 Doston ke liye (Kal Subah 7:00 AM):
     .scstatus 994402551176@s.whatsapp.net, 919876543210@s.whatsapp.net, 918765432109@s.whatsapp.net|0-7-9-10

B. WHATSAPP STATUS PRIVACY ("Only Share With...") KA RULE:
   Agar aapke WhatsApp me Status Privacy pehle se set hai:
   - 'contact' likhoge:
     Status sirf unhi logon ko dikhega jo WhatsApp Privacy me selected hain (Privacy safe rahegi).
   - Specific 'JID' likhoge:
     Status WhatsApp privacy ko chhodkar SIRF us ek JID wale bande ko dikhega (Secret Status).

C. CHECK KAISE KAREIN:
   .scstatus list

D. DISABLE / CANCEL KAISE KAREIN:
   .scstatus delete all
